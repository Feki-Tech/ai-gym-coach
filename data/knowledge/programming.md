# Programming basics

## Progressive overload
Progress comes from doing slightly more over time: another rep, a little
more weight (2.5 kg upper body, 5 kg lower body), one more set, or the same
work with better form and a slower eccentric. Change one variable per week.

## Reps, sets and intensity
Strength: 3-6 reps, 3-5 sets, long rests (2-4 min). Muscle growth
(hypertrophy): 6-15 reps, 3-4 sets, 60-120 s rest, stop 1-3 reps short of
failure on most sets. Endurance: 15+ reps, short rests. Beginners progress
well on 2-3 full-body sessions a week with 2-3 sets per exercise.

## Weekly volume per muscle group
Roughly 10-20 hard sets per muscle group per week works for most people;
start at the low end and add sets when progress stalls. Spread them over
2+ sessions.

## Rest between sessions
Give a muscle group about 48 hours before training it hard again. A simple
split: Monday full body, Wednesday full body, Friday full body — or
push / pull / legs across the week.

## Exercise order
Big compound lifts first (squat, deadlift, bench, press, pull-up) while you
are fresh, then accessories (curls, lunges, planks). Skill-heavy lifts
before fatiguing ones.

## Autoregulation with the app's data
Rep velocity loss over 20% in a set means the set is done — stop there to
keep form. Scores trending down across sets means the load is too heavy
today or rest was too short; drop the weight 5-10% or rest longer. A clean
set with all reps above 85 is a green light to add a rep or a little load
next session.

## Deloads
Every 4-8 weeks, or when scores and velocity drop for two sessions in a
row, take a lighter week: same exercises, 60-70% of the usual load and
sets.
