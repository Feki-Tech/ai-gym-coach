# Alternatives by equipment and constraint

## No equipment
Squat → bodyweight squat, split squat, wall sit. Bench → push-up, decline
push-up. Deadlift → single-leg Romanian deadlift (bodyweight), glute
bridge, hip hinge with a broomstick. Shoulder press → pike push-up.
Pull-up → doorframe rows, towel rows under a table. Curl → backpack curl
(load a bag with books).

## Dumbbells only
Goblet squat, dumbbell bench press, dumbbell Romanian deadlift, walking
lunges, dumbbell shoulder press, hammer curl, one-arm row.

## Bands only
Banded squat, banded push-up, banded good morning, banded row, banded
overhead press, banded curl, banded pull-apart for shoulder health.

## Knee pain (after a professional cleared you to train)
Prefer box squats to a high box, hip hinges (Romanian deadlift), glute
bridges, terminal knee extensions with a band; avoid deep lunges until
pain-free.

## Shoulder pain (after clearance)
Prefer neutral-grip presses, landmine press, rows, face pulls; avoid
behind-the-neck movements and wide-grip bench.

## Lower back pain (after clearance)
Prefer goblet squats, bird-dogs, dead bugs, glute bridges, light hinges
with a stick for feedback; avoid heavy deadlifts and loaded flexion.
