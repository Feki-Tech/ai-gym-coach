# Form faults the app detects — causes and fixes

## knees_cave — knee valgus (squat, lunge)
Knees drift inward on the way up. Usual causes: weak glutes / hip abductors,
poor foot pressure, load too heavy, fatigue. Cue: "screw your feet into the
floor, push the knees out over your toes". Build it: banded squats (band
above the knees), side-lying hip abductions, glute bridges, lateral band
walks; keep the whole foot planted. Reduce the load until the knees track
the toes on every rep.

## back_round — rounded lower back (deadlift, rows)
The spine flexes under load, most often at the bottom of a deadlift or when
the bar drifts away from the shins. Cue: "chest up, lats tight, bar close".
Set the back before the pull: brace the trunk (big breath into the belly),
pull the slack out of the bar, hinge from the hips. Lighten the load; use
blocks or a higher start (rack pull) until the setup holds.

## back_lean — excessive forward trunk lean (squat)
Torso pitches forward past ~50° from vertical. Causes: tight ankles, weak
quads or upper back, heels lifting. Cue: "chest proud, elbows under the
bar, sit between the heels". Try heel elevation (small plates or lifting
shoes), a slightly wider stance, and front-loaded variations (goblet or
front squat) which force an upright torso.

## torso_lean — torso leaning during lunges or curls
Lunge: keep the torso tall, the front shin roughly vertical, and step long
enough that the back knee drops straight down. Curl: a leaning torso means
the load is too heavy and momentum is doing the work — brace, lighten, and
own the full curl.

## lean_back — leaning back in the overhead press
Arching the lower back to press the bar "around" the face. Cue: "squeeze
the glutes, ribs down, push the head through once the bar passes the
forehead". Brace the core hard; reduce the load; press in a straight line.

## body_sag — hips sagging (plank, push-up)
The line shoulder–hip–ankle breaks at the hips. Cue: "squeeze the glutes
and abs, one straight line from head to heels". Shorten the hold or do
push-ups from an incline (hands on a bench) until the trunk holds.

## elbow_flare — elbows flared out (push-up, bench)
Upper arms at ~90° from the torso load the shoulder. Cue: "tuck the elbows
to about 45°, hands just outside the shoulders, wrists stacked under the
elbows". Think "bend the bar" or "arrow, not a T".

## elbow_swing — swinging the elbows (curls)
The upper arm moves forward and back to create momentum. Cue: "pin your
elbows to your ribs, curl with the biceps only". Lighter weight, slower
lowering (3 s), pause at the top.

## shallow — not reaching full depth / range of motion
Partial reps give less stimulus per rep and the app does not count them as
full depth. Cue: "own the bottom — hips below the knee crease for squats,
chest to fist-height on push-ups, full stretch on curls". Reduce the load,
add a 1-second pause at the bottom, and improve mobility (ankles for
squats, shoulders for presses) with 5 minutes of daily work.

## too_fast — rushed reps
A lift faster than the exercise's minimum concentric time means momentum,
not muscle. Cue: "control the way down (2-3 s), pause, then drive up". A
slower eccentric improves control and hypertrophy at the same load.

## uneven — sides moving differently (bench, press, pull-up)
One side lags or the bar tilts. Causes: asymmetry, grip width, a stronger
side compensating. Fix with unilateral work (single-arm dumbbell press,
single-arm rows), a symmetric grip checked against the bar knurling, and
lighter loads until both sides move together.

## chin — chin not over the bar (pull-up)
The rep stops short at the top. Cue: "drive the elbows down to the hips,
chest to the bar". Build with negatives (jump up, lower over 4-5 s),
band-assisted pull-ups, and heavy rows.

## shrug_neck — neck shrugged / head craned (plank, press)
Cue: "long neck, ears away from the shoulders, eyes on the floor a metre
ahead". Pull the shoulder blades down and slightly back.
