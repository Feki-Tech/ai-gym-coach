# Technique essentials per app exercise

## squat
Feet shoulder-width, toes slightly out, brace, sit down and back keeping
the chest up and the whole foot planted; hips below the knee crease at the
bottom, knees tracking over the toes; drive up through the mid-foot.
Camera: side or 45° front view.

## pushup
Hands slightly wider than the shoulders, body a straight line, elbows
tucked ~45°; lower the chest to about a fist from the floor, press back to
full lockout. Easier: hands on a bench. Harder: feet elevated, slow tempo.

## bench
Feet flat, shoulder blades pulled together and down, slight arch; bar to
the lower chest with elbows ~45-70°, press up and slightly back over the
shoulders. Always use safeties or a spotter with heavy loads.

## deadlift
Bar over mid-foot, shins to the bar, hips higher than knees, lats tight,
neutral spine; push the floor away, keep the bar against the legs, lock
out with the glutes; lower with a hip hinge, no rounding.

## lunge
Long step, torso tall, front shin roughly vertical, back knee drops
straight down to just above the floor; push through the front heel. Match
reps on both sides.

## shoulder_press
Bar or dumbbells at the collarbones, glutes and abs braced, ribs down;
press straight up, head "through the window" at the top; lower under
control. Avoid leaning back.

## curl
Elbows pinned to the ribs, wrists neutral; curl to full contraction,
lower over 2-3 seconds to full extension. No swinging or torso lean.

## pullup
Hands just outside shoulder width, start from a dead hang with the
shoulders packed; drive elbows down, chin over the bar; lower fully under
control. Use bands or negatives to build up.

## plank
Elbows under shoulders, glutes and abs squeezed, one straight line head to
heels, long neck; breathe. Aim for perfect 20-40 s holds rather than long
sagging ones.
