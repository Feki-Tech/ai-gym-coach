# Nutrition basics for training

## Protein
About 1.6-2.2 g of protein per kg of bodyweight per day, spread over 3-4
meals, supports muscle growth and recovery. Sources: eggs, dairy, fish,
poultry, meat, tofu, tempeh, legumes, whey.

## Energy
To gain muscle, eat a small surplus (200-300 kcal above maintenance); to
lose fat, a moderate deficit (300-500 kcal) while keeping protein high and
training hard so muscle is kept.

## Around training
A meal with carbohydrates and protein 1-3 hours before training gives
energy; another within a couple of hours after helps recovery. Timing
matters far less than the daily total.

## Hydration
Drink to thirst plus a little extra on training days; 500 ml around a
session is typical. Dark urine or a headache after training suggests you
are under-hydrated.

## Supplements
Creatine monohydrate (3-5 g daily) is the best-supported strength
supplement; caffeine before training helps performance. Everything else is
optional. Not medical advice — check with a professional if you have a
condition or take medication.
